Full description:
Find matching animal friends and explore a little more with every stage.

Little Match Adventure is an illustrated matching game for young children. Turn over two tiles, remember the animals, and find each pair at your own pace.

FOUR PLACES TO EXPLORE
Discover the meadow, forest, ocean, and safari, with original animal illustrations and a different background in every world.

80 MATCHING STAGES
Start with small boards and work through 20 stages in each world. Animated celebrations mark each completed stage.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Switch gentle background music and button sounds on or off separately. Saved stages and an unfinished board let you return to your adventure on the same device. Gameplay works offline; advertisements require an internet connection.

11 LANGUAGES
Play in English, Spanish, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Portuguese, French, Japanese, German, or Korean.

ADVERTISING
The Android app contains ads between stages after every three completed games, and optional rewarded ads for hints. Ads are requested with child-directed and non-personalized settings. No account or in-app purchases are required.
