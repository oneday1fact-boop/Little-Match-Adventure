Little Match Adventure Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

This policy covers Little Match Adventure, Android package com.littlematch.adventure, and its web game. For support, privacy questions, or a request concerning your child's information, contact the Little Match Adventure developer at oneday1fact@gmail.com.

Children and gameplay

The game is intended for children aged 8 and under. It does not require an account or ask players to provide a name, email address, birthday, precise location, photos, contacts, microphone recordings, or payment details. There is no chat, user-generated content, or in-app purchasing.

Saved progress

Completed stages, an unfinished board, language, and sound preferences are stored on the device. We do not run a server that receives this gameplay save. Android may back up or transfer this local data according to your device and Google account settings. Clear the app's storage in Android Settings to reset the local save; manage backups separately in your device/account settings. Browser saves can be removed by clearing site data.

Advertising in the Android app

The Android app uses Google's AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and User Messaging Platform. Ads may appear between stages after every three completed games. A hint can be earned by choosing to view a rewarded ad. If an ad is unavailable, gameplay continues and a hint is available without an ad.

All ad requests are configured as child-directed and under the age of consent, with non-personalized ads and a maximum G ad-content rating. The app removes Android advertising-ID and advertising-services ID, attribution, and topics permissions. These settings do not mean that no data is processed by Google.

Google's SDKs may send IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device/app information and identifiers, advertising interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostics such as crashes and performance information to Google. This supports ad delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and legal compliance. We do not add our own analytics SDK or a mediation network. Non-personalized ads can still use data for these purposes. We do not use the game to build behavioral advertising profiles of children.

Google processes SDK data under its own policies and retention practices. The developer cannot promise deletion of Google's records. For details see Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy), partner-site/app data use (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and advertising controls (https://myadcenter.google.com/). A parent or guardian may contact us about privacy concerns; do not send a child's personal information unnecessarily.

Web game

The web game uses labeled demonstration ad previews rather than the native AdMob SDK. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process standard request information such as IP addresses for delivering and securing the site, under its own privacy policy. Gameplay assets and saves can be cached locally for offline play.

Security, choices, and changes

The app uses HTTPS for network services. No network or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Gameplay can be used offline; advertising requires a network connection. We do not sell players' personal information. To ask about this policy, contact oneday1fact@gmail.com. If practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.
